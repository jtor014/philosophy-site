About the Series

The Idea

The Eternal Argument is a podcast that tells the story of human thought from its earliest beginnings to the present day. It covers philosophy, but not as a dry catalogue of names and dates. Each episode is a story about people wrestling with the hardest questions: What is real? What is good? What are we?

The Structure: An Inverted Pyramid

The series is designed like an inverted pyramid. It starts fast and broad, then slows down and deepens as it approaches the present.

The early eras sweep through centuries in a handful of episodes, giving you the essential foundations. By the later eras, we slow to a crawl, spending entire episodes on a single book or a single argument. The idea is simple: you need the basics before you can go deep.
